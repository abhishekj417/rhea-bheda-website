Wheel of Life

See the whole picture before choosing what comes next.

A simple reflection tool can reveal where life feels aligned, where it feels depleted, and which change deserves your attention first.

A reflective starting point

Balance is personal, not perfect.

The Wheel of Life invites you to rate how satisfied you currently feel across important areas. The purpose is not to achieve identical scores. It is to notice what your current pattern is telling you.
