About Rhea

Transformation with warmth, honesty and ownership.

Coach · facilitator · guide

Helping you return to your own clarity.

Rhea Bheda is a Transformation Coach, Yoga & Meditation Facilitator, and creator of the E3 Framework.

Based in Dubai and working with clients globally, Rhea supports high-potential people navigating high-pressure lives. Her approach brings together self-awareness, emotional ownership and intentional evolution.

The purpose is not to hand you a perfect formula. It is to help you see yourself without judgment, understand what is shaping your choices, and build the inner capacity to choose differently.

What I believe

The person you are becoming deserves your conscious participation.

Real growth is not performance. It is alignment between your inner world, daily choices and the life you are building.

  • Your emotions carry information, not instructions.
  • Awareness without ownership rarely creates lasting change.
  • Self-acceptance and ambition can exist together.
  • Small, conscious choices shape identity over time.
The work

From knowing to becoming.

Many people already know what they should do. The challenge is understanding why they do not consistently do it, and learning how to act from a more grounded identity.

That is the space Rhea’s work explores through coaching, reflection, yoga and meditation practices.
