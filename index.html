Frequently asked questions

A little clarity before you begin.

What is transformation coaching?+

It is a reflective, action-oriented process that helps you notice the beliefs, emotions and patterns shaping your choices, then practise more conscious ways of responding.

Who is Rhea’s work designed for?+

Rhea’s work is for high-potential people navigating high-pressure lives who want more clarity, emotional ownership and alignment between what they know and how they live.

What is the E3 Framework?+

E3 stands for Embrace, Empower and Evolve: seeing yourself honestly, owning your choices without blame, and turning insight into aligned action.

Is coaching the same as therapy?+

No. Coaching is not a substitute for psychotherapy, medical care or emergency mental-health support. If you need clinical support, contact a qualified healthcare professional.

Can I work with Rhea if I am outside Dubai?+

Yes. Rhea is based in Dubai and works with clients globally. Exact session formats and availability will be confirmed during an initial conversation.

How do I get started?+

Begin with the starting-point check-in, try the E3 practice, or explore the Wheel of Life. When Rhea’s verified contact details are added, you will also be able to enquire about coaching directly.
