Embrace

The emotion is real. The first interpretation may not be.

Before acting, name what you feel and ask what story you are attaching to it. That pause can reveal choices that a quick reaction hides.

02
Empower

Ownership is not blame.

You can acknowledge what happened to you without making it your fault. Ownership asks a different question: what response is available to me now?

03
Evolve

Identity is rehearsed through action.

You do not need to feel completely ready before you act. A small aligned choice gives your future identity something real to stand on.
