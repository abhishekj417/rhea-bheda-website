Coach Rhea’s approach

E3 guides the work.

E3 is Rhea’s framework for moving from automatic patterns to conscious practice.

01

Embrace what is present

Slow down enough to notice the feeling, need, belief or pattern shaping the moment.

02

Empower your choice

Separate what you cannot control from the boundary, decision or response that belongs to you.

03

Evolve through practice

Choose one realistic action and keep practising the response you want to make more natural.

Is coaching right for you?

Coaching is most useful when you are willing to be curious, honest and responsible for the choices you make between conversations.

Coaching is distinct from medical care, psychotherapy and emergency mental-health support. If you need clinical support, contact a qualified healthcare professional.
